<p>Arent’s purpose is “Solving construction industry challenges with the power of technology, together,” and our mission is to “democratize tacit knowledge” (listed on the TSE Growth Market). Our DX business covers everything from consulting to system development and commercialization, and our product business offers “GENBA Dock” (an AI docking platform for the construction industry) and “PlantStream” (3D CAD with automatic routing for plant piping design). GENBA Dock includes “GENBA Dock BIM AI Agent” (an add-in in which AI operates Revit) and “GENBA Dock Schedule Management (PROCOLLA)” (an AI-powered cloud schedule management system).<br /><br />100% of our developers work remotely (as of May 2026), and some members work fully remotely from regional areas outside Tokyo.
